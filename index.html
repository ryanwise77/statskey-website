The hardest signal to talk about

The gut: huge, multifactorial, and mostly invisible.

Around 40% of people have a disorder of gut–brain interaction and ~12% have IBS; most name food as a trigger, but every trigger list is individual. In one US study, more than 60% of patients hid bowel-urgency symptoms from their own doctor out of embarrassment.

StatsKey's Gut Check records the whole episode — Bristol type with mixed-episode phases, urgency, control, passage, cleanup, red flags — and scores each one 0–10 for GI burden, right alongside food, fiber, glucose, and training. Gut–brain patterns compute from your own paired days, the record locks behind Face ID, and Intelligence can trace any rough afternoon back to the meals before it. No one has to say a word.
