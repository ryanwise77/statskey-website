The bid is in the architecture

Price every consequence. Hide none.

A low sticker fee is not a low-cost system. StatsKey counts software, operator, sponsor, loss, returns, disputes, liquidity, operations, transition, payer friction, and public externalities before it calls a route cheaper.

One control plane

The lifecycle, not a checkout button.

The sandbox exercises the software authority boundary across the payment lifecycle. Live delivery still requires contracted financial institutions, processors, rail access, compliance operations, certification, and liquidity.

01

Authorize

Intent binding, payer authority, risk evidence, sanctions gates, and idempotency.

02

Route

Eligibility before ranking, bounded costs, deterministic choice, and incumbent fallback.

03

Account

Immutable balanced journals, holds, captures, fees, reversals, and provisional credit.

04

Settle

Clearing instructions, liquidity gates, finality evidence, and exception-safe recovery.

05

Reconcile

External evidence ingestion, unknown-state queries, breaks, aging, and operator cases.

06

Resolve

Returns, disputes, refunds, token compromise, audit trails, and consumer recourse.

Sponsor proposal

First United, by proposal—not assumption.

StatsKey proposes First United Bank and Trust Company of Durant, Oklahoma as the sponsor bank for a governed program. The proposal is to place deposits, bank-core balances, liquidity, compliance, and settlement controls with the regulated institution while StatsKey supplies the decision and evidence software.

No relationship is represented. This build does not claim sponsorship, live accounts, rail access, licensed acquiring, approvals, production credentials, or settlement authority.
