How it works

Permission stays with the person sharing.

Care Sharing is deliberately small: one person, one scoped record, one professional account, one expiration.

They choose what to share.

In the iOS or Android app, the person selects exactly which categories and dates to share, names the intended professional or organization, and confirms the disclosure.

You redeem it once.

A one-time code binds the record to your verified email and professional account. The code cannot be reused by another account.

Access ends cleanly.

The person who shared the record can revoke access immediately. Otherwise it closes automatically on the selected expiration date.

Designed with a boundary

Useful wellness context, clearly separated from clinical records.

StatsKey is a consumer wellness record. The professional view keeps its source and coverage visible instead of presenting it as verified clinical documentation.

What can be shared

  • Daily activity and sleep totals
  • Nutrition totals without meal names or notes
  • Workout summaries without routes or sensor streams
  • Vitals, glucose summaries, body metrics, and blood panels
  • Coverage and source information for every category

What StatsKey Care is not

  • Not an emergency or alerting service
  • Not continuous monitoring
  • Not an EHR, order system, or verified lab feed
  • Not a substitute for professional judgment or clinical assessment
  • Not a place for treatment instructions or messaging
