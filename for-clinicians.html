How it works

Permission stays with the patient.

Care Sharing is deliberately small: one patient, one scoped record, one professional account, one expiration.

The patient chooses.

In the iOS or Android app, the patient selects exactly which categories and dates to share, names the intended office, and confirms the disclosure.

You redeem once.

A one-time code binds the record to your verified email and professional portal. The code cannot be reused by another account.

Access ends cleanly.

The patient can revoke the share immediately. Otherwise it closes automatically on the selected expiration date.

Designed with a boundary

Useful context without pretending to be an EHR.

StatsKey is a consumer wellness record. The portal keeps that provenance visible instead of presenting it as verified clinical documentation.

What the patient can share

  • Daily activity and sleep totals
  • Nutrition totals without meal names or notes
  • Workout summaries without routes or sensor streams
  • Vitals, glucose summaries, body metrics, and blood panels
  • Coverage and source information for every category

What this portal is not

  • Not an emergency or alerting service
  • Not continuous patient monitoring
  • Not an EHR, order system, or verified lab feed
  • Not a substitute for clinical assessment
  • Not a place for treatment instructions or messaging
