Overview

This sheet turns the CBM platform's dimensioned bill of materials into an orderable procurement view, grouped by build track. Every line carries the exact part number, the modeled or datasheet dimensions, the vendor, an indicative price range, a status, and a source link.

The bench prototype is fully catalog-orderable today at roughly $3,300–$7,200: a benchtop potentiostat, the 8-well screen-printed gold array, reference and counter electrodes, and controllers — enough to bring up the full panel chemistry with zero custom fabrication. The reusable puck, disposable cartridge, and chemistry tracks are largely catalog reagents and components.

A handful of RFQ-only custom items — the microneedle sensing head, the sensing flex / interposer, the VARTA CoinPower battery, and the PalmSens puck PCB — have no committed catalog price until a vendor quotes them. Where a figure is shown for an RFQ line it is indicative only.

Prices are indicative planning ranges from BOM_master_dimensioned.csv. Vendor quotes, quantity breaks, tax, and shipping are excluded. Dimensions are datasheet/package values (real) or modeled design targets (noted per row).

8Notes & boundaries

  • Prices are indicative planning ranges from the dimensioned BOM (BOM_master_dimensioned.csv); vendor quotes, quantity breaks, tax, and shipping are excluded.
  • Dimensions are either datasheet/package values (real) or modeled design targets from the CAD (noted per row); printed and custom parts are subject to first-fit measurement.
  • RFQ items have no committed catalog price until a vendor quotes; any figure shown for an RFQ line is indicative only.
  • The two printed shells reference a local CAD file (panel_cartridge.scad) and are produced in-house rather than ordered from a vendor.
  • “In hand” parts are already on the bench and carry no incremental purchase cost for the first build.
