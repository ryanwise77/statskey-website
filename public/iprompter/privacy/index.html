iPrompter Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026

In short: iPrompter does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Your scripts and recordings stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Information Collection

iPrompter Studio by StatsKey does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data-collection SDKs. The app does not send scripts, recordings, device identifiers, usage information, diagnostics, or personal information to StatsKey servers.

Scripts and Settings

Scripts and prompter preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple-provided storage. They are not uploaded by iPrompter. Removing the app may remove this locally stored information.

Camera, Microphone, and Photos

With your permission, iPrompter uses the camera and microphone only to create the video you choose to record. A temporary recording file is created on your device and then saved to your Photos library. iPrompter requests add-only Photos access for this purpose. StatsKey does not receive or retain your camera, microphone, script, or recording content.

Purchases

Apple processes the one-time App Store purchase. iPrompter does not receive your payment-card information. Apple may provide developers with aggregated sales and financial reports under Apple's own privacy terms.

Sharing

iPrompter does not share data automatically. If you use Apple system features to share or publish a finished recording, that action is initiated and controlled by you and is governed by the receiving service's privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Because iPrompter does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If iPrompter's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions about iPrompter privacy can be sent to ryanws@statskeybiometrics.com.
