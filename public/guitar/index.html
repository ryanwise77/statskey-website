Engineering Concept

One guitar, engineered to cover the whole map.

This is a personal design study: a single hybrid instrument that aims to deliver believable acoustic textures, expressive single-coil voices, and thick humbucker rhythm and leads, on one neck, built to survive stage use. Every option below is a true-scale, component-level 3D model generated from the same millimeter specification, not a sketch.

Tone is described directly in sonic terms. Pick the direction that matches the sound in your head.

Design intent
The default direction is the Deeper Chambered Hardtail, offered in two magnetic voicings: a single-coil-forward HSS build for maximum brightness and acoustic-leaning breadth, and an HH / HSH build with coil-splits and a mahogany/maple body for thick rhythm and warm, sustaining leads. Both share the same hybrid piezo and body-sensor acoustic layer, dual/stereo output, and serviceable electronics, so one instrument can move from driving rhythm to warm leads to amplified acoustic textures.
