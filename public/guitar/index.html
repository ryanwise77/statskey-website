Engineering Concept

An acoustic-first hybrid, engineered at true scale.

TX-A22 is a personal design study built around a Grand Auditorium-class acoustic body, electric-neck ergonomics, a non-invasive soundhole magnetic pickup, undersaddle and internal sensing, blend controls, and independent acoustic and magnetic outputs.
