Clean-Sheet Acoustic-First Study

TX-A22 is the acoustic-first path, but it is not a build option yet.

This keeps the idea alive without pretending the previous CAD was possible. The goal is an Acoustasonic/Godin-like rock hybrid for convincing acoustic-rock and atmospheric clean material, with electric support added only after the soundboard, bridge load path, and air volume make sense.

Side Section

Design Rules

Soundboard First

The spruce top, bridge plate, braces, and saddle load path define whether this can do credible acoustic strumming. They must be voiced before pickup routing.

Magnetic Pickup Last

One low-profile magnetic support pickup is allowed, but it must not rout through the active top area or dominate the design.

Soundport Must Be Real

The port needs a coupled upper air path. If it is not coupled, it should be removed rather than used as decoration.

Prototype Honesty

This page is a direction and constraint model. A luthier must review cross-sections before it becomes a 3D build option.

What This Is For

Strong

  • Dramatic acoustic-rock parts
  • Modern acoustic songs
  • Atmospheric clean/acoustic textures
  • Fingerstyle and open strumming

Acceptable

  • Layered electric/acoustic studio parts
  • Moderate driven rock rhythm
  • Band use with acoustic IR/preamp

Not The Goal

  • Full high-gain authority
  • HSH switching
  • Real single-coil neck-pickup tones
  • High-gain feedback-proof electric work
