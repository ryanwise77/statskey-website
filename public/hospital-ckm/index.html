Hospital continuous potassium monitoring

A hospital-grade CKM is not the wearable.

Hospital CKM uses a closed inline flow cell or drawn-line micro-sample path for ICU, dialysis, CRRT, DKA, insulin, and post-cardiac-surgery workflows. It is built for treatment-grade trend surveillance between blood gas or laboratory checks.

Research status: prototype render and architecture only. Not a medical device, not sterile, and not for patient care.

Differentiation

Wearable CKM trends ISF. Hospital CKM measures near the clinical fluid path.

The wearable product is an outpatient, lab-confirmation trigger. The hospital product is a bedside surveillance module meant to sit beside existing POC analyzers and line-based workflows.

01 / SAMPLE

Direct fluid advantage.

Blood, dialysate, CRRT, or controlled micro-samples reduce the ISF-to-serum validation burden that limits wearable claims.

02 / CALIBRATION

Hospital-grade controls.

Larger references, thermostatted membranes, onboard standards, blank cycles, and paired blood-gas checks create analytical headroom.

03 / WORKFLOW

Between-sample trend.

The claim is not autonomous dosing. It is faster recognition of dangerous potassium movement between scheduled samples.

Validation path

Build toward POC/lab agreement, not wearable convenience.

BenchKCl standards, whole-blood simulants, dialysate/CRRT matrices, temperature sweeps, protein/fouling challenges, and flow artifacts.
ComparatorPaired i-STAT, blood gas, central lab, and dialysis-machine chemistry where available.
SafetyClosed disposable path, hemolysis/clotting/fouling checks, pressure drop, infection-control review, and fail-closed output.
Use casesInsulin/DKA potassium shifts, CRRT, dialysis transition, post-cardiac-surgery monitoring, diuretic titration, refeeding, transplant induction.
