Hospital CKM

Bedside potassium monitoring without wearable constraints.

Hospital CKM is the treatment-grade branch: a closed inline flow-cell for ICU, dialysis, CRRT, and step-down workflows. It is separate from Wearable CKM, which trends dermal ISF and triggers confirmatory labs.
